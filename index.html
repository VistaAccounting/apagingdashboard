How these numbers are worked out
  • Open balance = voucher amount − paid − discount, from the Famous “Unpaid Vouchers” exports for Road 156 (ROAD), Vista Vineyards Operating (VVO) and Vista Vineyards Sales (VVS). Credits are included as negatives.
  • Aging buckets count days from invoice date to Nov 13, 2026. Past due means the due date is before Nov 13; “due by Nov 20” covers Nov 13 through Nov 20.
  • Categories are provisional: no vendor lookup file was uploaded this week, so they were assigned from vendor names and voucher descriptions.
  • Vendors are grouped by exact name across companies; possible duplicates are listed, not merged.
